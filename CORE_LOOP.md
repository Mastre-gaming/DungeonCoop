## Tensions to settle in the paper fight
1. **The pace.** A 4.5-minute round in a 7-minute fight is about 1.5 rounds per fight, too few for a set-up-then-finish combo. Both friends also asked for faster turns, and move + action + bonus action adds time per turn. Measure a real round on paper. If it runs past about 2 minutes, try the team turn or a turn timer before cutting actions.
2. **Permanent levels and "every run starts weak".** Fraps's « de pouilleux à hyper chauds » fits a rise within each run. Permanent levels make each run start stronger, and a friend who misses evenings falls behind. A split to test: levels carry over and widen your options, while gear, gold and run abilities reset.
3. **The size of a run.** About 65 minutes at about 7 minutes a fight fits roughly 6 fights plus events, rests, a shop and a boss: a map of about 8 to 10 rooms, not the pitch's 10 to 15.
